Plants for every windowsill

We grow herbs, houseplants and small trees in our glasshouse at the foot of the hill, and sell them straight from the bench. Every plant is potted by hand and comes with a card that explains how much light and water it likes.

New to plants? Ask us. We would rather help you choose one that suits your room than sell you one that struggles.
