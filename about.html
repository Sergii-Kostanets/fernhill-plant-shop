About the shop

Two friends started Fernhill in 2009 with one glasshouse, a wheelbarrow and a lot of seed trays. Today the shop has three glasshouses and a small team, and most of what we sell still starts life from a seed or a cutting here on the hill.

How we grow

We keep things simple, and we explain every choice on the card that comes with the plant.

  • Peat-free compost in every pot
  • Rainwater from our own tanks
  • No plastic labels, only card
